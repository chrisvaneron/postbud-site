Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

PostBud is an iPhone and iPad app that turns the video clips you pick into one finished video. It is made and operated by AI Daily Dose LLC, a New York limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy says what PostBud collects, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. Questions: admin@useaidailydose.com.

The short version.
  • Your original clips stay in your Photos library. PostBud never uploads them.
  • To choose a cut, PostBud sends a small, low-resolution preview of your clips, their audio, and a still image of each clip to our servers, and from there to AI services that transcribe speech and help pick the cut.
  • We ask for your agreement in the app before anything is sent.
  • No ads. No tracking across apps or websites. We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete any video, or your whole account, in the app at any time.

3. How we use information

  • To provide PostBud: transcribe, choose the cut, write captions, apply your edits, and send your device the instructions to build the video.
  • To run accounts, plans and the monthly video allowance.
  • To notify you when a cut is ready, if you allow notifications.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To improve how well Bud cuts videos, using measurements and records of edits. We do not use your footage to train our own AI models without asking you first.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. How long we keep it

  • Uploaded previews, audio, stills and working files for a video: until you delete the video or your account. We may delete them automatically sooner, about 14 days after a video's last activity on the free plan and about 60 days on a paid plan. Clips uploaded to a video whose cut was never started may be deleted after about 48 hours.
  • The cut plan, transcript and edit history of a video: up to about one year, or until you delete the video or your account.
  • Account and purchase records: while your account exists. Records we must keep for tax, accounting or legal reasons are kept as long as the law requires.
  • Diagnostic logs: only as long as we need them to fix problems and keep the service secure, and no longer than your account exists.

Deleting a video in the app removes that video's uploaded files and records from our servers. Deleting your account removes every video, file and record we hold for you, and your sign-in. Videos saved to your Photos library are yours and are not affected. Backups and the AI services' short-term logs may take a little longer to clear.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete: delete a video, or your account, in the app (tap Bud on the home screen, then Delete account), or email us.
  • Access, copy, correct: email us and we will tell you what we hold and give you a copy, or correct it.
  • Notifications and Photos access: change these in iOS Settings at any time. PostBud works with limited Photos access.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in your Apple account settings.

We answer requests within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.
