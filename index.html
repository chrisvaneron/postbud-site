What leaves your phone, and what doesn't.

Stays on your phone

  • Your original clips. They stay in your Photos library.
  • The finished video. Your phone makes it and saves it to Photos. We never receive it.

Sent so Bud can pick the cut

  • A small, low-resolution preview of each clip you pick, its audio, and one still image.
  • AI services use these to transcribe what you said and choose the cut.
  • Delete a video and its files are removed from our servers.

The Privacy Policy lists every service and how long files are kept.

Good to know.

Does PostBud post for me?

No. Your phone saves the finished video to Photos, and you post it on TikTok, Reels or Shorts yourself, with whatever caption you like.

What does it run on?

iPhone and iPad.

Do my clips leave my phone?

Your originals never do. PostBud sends a small, low-resolution preview, the audio and a still image of each clip you pick so Bud can choose the cut. The Privacy Policy has the details.

What counts as a video?

A video counts when you start its cut, not when you pick clips. Cutting it again, editing the parts, fixing captions and adding a voiceover do not use another one.

Can I change what Bud picked?

Yes. Move, split or take out any part, fix a caption, add a voiceover, and build again.
